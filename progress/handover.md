## Accounts and where secrets live

Never commit secrets. The values live only in the services below and in each developer's local `.env` files (git ignored).

| Service | Used for | Where its secrets go |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neon (Postgres) | Database: project `cool-smoke-27052649`, branch `production` for staging | `DATABASE_URL` (pooled), `DIRECT_URL` (direct) in Render |
| Upstash (Redis) | Seat holds, rate limits, live positions, BullMQ | `REDIS_URL` in Render |
| Render | `aptransit-api` and `aptransit-worker` (WORKER=1) | Render environment |
| Vercel | Web app, root `apps/web` | Vercel environment (`API_URL`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_*`) |
| Razorpay (test mode) | Payments and refunds | `RAZORPAY_*` in Render, `NEXT_PUBLIC_RAZORPAY_KEY_ID` in Vercel |
| Resend | Email OTP and notifications | `RESEND_API_KEY` in Render |
| Generated secrets | `JWT_SECRET`, `OTP_PEPPER`, `QR_SIGNING_PRIVATE_KEY`, `QR_SECRET_KEY` | Render only. Rotate anything ever pasted into a chat or ticket (docs/12) |

## Costs and limits observed

| Service | Free tier limit | Observed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neon | 0.5 GB storage, compute hours per month | 14 day history is about 750,000 rows; measure storage after the staging seed |
| Upstash | 500,000 commands per month per database | Worker drain delay 60 s keeps idle polling low (docs/15); measure after a week on staging |
| Render | Free instances sleep after 15 minutes idle | First request after sleep takes about a minute |
| Vercel | Hobby plan | Not measured yet |

Staging was not deployed during the 20 days on this machine; the numbers above need a week of staging to fill in.
